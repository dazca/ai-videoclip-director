## Notes and caveats

- CC0 covers copyright only. Poly Haven location views show public streets and buildings; no people are visible in
  the chosen crops, but a parked car may show part of a number plate. Blur it if you use the plate in a published shot.
- Poly Haven's *website* (logos, page renders, text) is not CC0; only the downloadable assets are. We used asset files only.
- The Dürer plates show schematic unclothed figures (tagged `unclothed-schematic`); the UI may hide that tag by default.
- The 3D bodies are synthetic (not scans of real people). No item depicts an identifiable living person.
